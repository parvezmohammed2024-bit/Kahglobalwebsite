Institutional FAQs

Frequently Asked Quotation Questions

Transparent purchasing terms for Malaysian corporations, factories, and institutions.

help_outline What is the standard payment term for corporate clients?

Standard orders require a 50% confirmation deposit to initiate fabric dye-cutting, with the remaining 50% upon final delivery inspection. Registered government entities, universities, and established public listed corporations may apply for 30-day corporate credit terms upon SSM verification.

help_outline Can we receive a pre-production sample before bulk sewing?

Yes. For all customized orders exceeding 100 pieces, Kah Global produces and couriers one full pre-production physical prototype with complete embroidery/printing for your management committee's physical sign-off before commencing bulk assembly.

help_outline What file formats are preferred for company logos?

We recommend vector formats (Adobe Illustrator .AI, .EPS, or vector .PDF) to preserve razor-sharp thread lines. If you only possess a raster PNG or JPEG, our Cheras graphic prepress unit will vectorize and optimize stitch coordinates free of charge.

help_outline Do you handle nationwide delivery to multiple subsidiary branches?

Absolutely. We manage multi-destination logistics across West and East Malaysia. Garments are polybagged, labeled by department or employee name, and bundled into master cartons with detailed packing slips for straightforward branch distribution.
