## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, industrial-grade B2B presence tailored for institutional apparel procurement, corporate uniform programs, and contract textile manufacturing. The visual aesthetic projects reliability, manufacturing precision, and operational dependability.

The core audience consists of procurement managers, corporate HR directors, and industrial operations leads who require assurance of fabric durability, timely bulk production runs, and transparent tier pricing. The interface evokes stability and certified craftsmanship through heavy, deliberate visual grounding: deep navy structural anchors, precise high-contrast typography, and purposeful high-visibility industrial orange accents reserved strictly for functional conversion actions.

Drawing from modern technical minimalism and structural enterprise design, layouts prioritize clarity, specifications density, and institutional trust signals without ornamental distraction.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for high-contrast legibility and procurement authority.

- **Primary (`#0B2545`):** Deep Navy anchors navigation, primary headline hierarchy, data table headers, and structural UI elements. It conveys institutional permanence and industrial scale.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#F26B1D`):** Industrial Safety Orange functions strictly as an action driver for quote requests, lead captures, sample kit ordering, and key status callouts. It is applied sparingly to retain strong visual tension.
- **Tertiary (`#134074`):** Steel Navy provides surface hierarchy, active toggle fills, and structural sub-borders within data-dense sections.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Base White (`#FFFFFF`): Primary background for content containers and product cards.
  - Surface Accent (`#F4F6F8`): Low-glare cool grey for page backdrops, table zebra striping, and specification blocks.
  - Border Slate (`#E2E8F0`): Clean, structural dividers and input perimeter boundaries.
  - Body Text Slate (`#1E293B`): Deep neutral ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against white and light grey backgrounds.
  - Muted Neutral (`#64748B`): Secondary descriptors, metadata labels, and measurement units (e.g., GSM ratings, RM currency markers).

## Typography

The type scale combines the geometric confidence of Manrope for titles and headlines with the mechanical precision and tabular legibility of Inter for specifications, data, and body copy.

- **Headlines (Manrope):** High geometric precision, tight letter-tracking, and robust weights (600–800) create commanding focal anchors for industrial certifications, fabric titles, and value metrics.
- **Body & Tabular Copy (Inter):** Highly legible at all sizes with neutral character shapes. Tabular numbers (`tnum`) must be enabled on all pricing tiers (RM values), minimum order quantities (MOQ), and fabric composition percentages.
- **Labels & Overlines (Inter Semibold):** Micro-copy, badge chips, and field indicators use uppercase letter-spaced configurations to maintain clear legibility at 12px and 14px sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an 8px modular spacing rhythm on an asymmetric 12-column fluid grid, scaling down to a 4-column system on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns with a max content container of 1280px, 24px gutters, and 48px outer canvas margin.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px canvas margins.

Generous vertical section spacing (`space-2xl`) reinforces enterprise-grade quality and reduces cognitive fatigue during product comparisons and technical specification audits.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through subtle ambient elevation layered over structural slate borders (`#E2E8F0`). Flat cards are defined by clean outlines, while elevated interactive cards use low-opacity, blue-tinted shadows to prevent a generic grey wash.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0; background: #FFFFFF;` Used for embedded spec sheets, static data cards, and input fields.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.05); border: 1px solid #E2E8F0;` Used for catalog apparel cards, client logo blocks, and summary widgets.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Focus):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.04); border: 1px solid #CBD5E1; transform: translateY(-2px);` Used on interactive manufacturing catalog cards and active quotation steps.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Modals):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08);` Reserved for RFQ (Request for Quote) slide-outs, spec magnification modals, and swatch selectors.

## Shapes

The design uses a 12px standard corner radius for primary interaction containers, striking a balance between industrial utility and modern corporate aesthetics.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standard 12px corner radius (`0.75rem` / rounded-lg token under level 2 setting).
- **Buttons, Field Inputs, Badges:** 8px corner radius (`0.5rem`) for compact structural alignment.
- **Pills / Status Chips:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) used strictly for live status indicators (e.g., "Ready Stock", "ISO 9001 Certified", "Custom Dyeing Available").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Quotation / RFQ):** Background `#F26B1D`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 8px, font-weight 600. On hover: background `#D95A13`. Focus: ring 3px of `#F26B1D` at 30% opacity.
- **Secondary Action (Catalogue Download / Technical Specs):** Background `#0B2545`, text `#FFFFFF`. On hover: background `#134074`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, text `#0B2545`. On hover: background `#F4F6F8`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Specification Chips (GSM, Fabric Composition):** Background `#F4F6F8`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`, font size 12px, font-weight 600.
- **Industrial Accents (MOQ, Certifications):** Background `#FEF3EC`, border `#FCD8C1`, text `#C24C0A`.

### Cards
- **Product & Solution Cards:** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`), 12px corner radius, 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`. Padding uses `space-lg` (24px). Image frame container sits flush to top edge with a 4:3 technical aspect ratio and light grey baseline (`#F4F6F8`).
- **Quote Matrix Cards:** Light grey container (`#F4F6F8`), structured with inner 1px slate divider lines, featuring clear currency labels (`RM [Amount] / unit`).

### Input Fields & Selects
- 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 8px corner radius, background `#FFFFFF`, typography Inter 14px.
- Height: 44px for standard inputs; 48px for primary quotation wizard fields to maximize touch accuracy on mobile and tablet sales devices.
- Focus state: border `#0B2545`, outer ring 2px with `#0B2545` at 15% opacity.

### Data Tables & Spec Sheets
- Header: `#0B2545` deep navy background, `#FFFFFF` crisp uppercase text, 12px Inter semibold with 0.05em tracking.
- Row striping: alternating `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
- Cell borders: 1px bottom border `#E2E8F0`.
- Numeric alignment: Right-aligned with tabular figures for price points (`RM`) and lead-time schedules.

### Trust & Credibility Footprint
- Localized badge patterns integrating factory location markers (Cheras, Kuala Lumpur), compliance standards (SIRIM, Oeko-Tex), and direct factory capacity counters.
