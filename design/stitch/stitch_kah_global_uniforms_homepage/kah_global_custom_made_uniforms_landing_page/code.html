Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tailoring custom uniforms with our Cheras plant.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom-made uniforms? expand_more
Our minimum order quantity starts at just 50 pieces per design and colourway. For orders below 50 pieces, please consult our ready-stock catalogue which supports custom embroidery and silkscreen customization starting from 20 pieces.
Can you match our company's exact corporate Pantone colour code? expand_more
Yes. For orders of 100 pieces and above, our factory laboratory performs precision lab-dip matching to achieve a Delta-E < 0.5 match against your Pantone TCX or PMS standard. For urgent 50-piece rush runs, we maintain over 80+ in-stock shade reels in our Cheras warehouse.
Do you provide a physical sample before bulk cutting begins? expand_more
Always. Once design specifications and tech packs are approved, we sew a full physical pre-production prototype complete with actual embroidery, custom collar piping, and chosen buttons within 3 to 5 working days. Mass bulk cutting begins only after your management signs off on this physical prototype.
How long does the entire custom manufacturing process take? expand_more
The standard end-to-end timeline is 10 to 14 working days: 3-5 days for physical sample sign-off, followed by 7-10 days for bulk CAD cutting, assembly line sewing, Tajima embroidery, and quality inspection. Expedited rush production (5-7 days) is available upon request for critical corporate events.
Can we visit your showroom and manufacturing floor in Cheras? expand_more
We welcome corporate procurement visits. Our factory showroom at Industrial Park Cheras houses over 500+ past client uniform samples, complete fabric swatch reels, and allows you to inspect our computerized Tajima embroidery lines in real time. We are open Monday to Friday from 8:30am – 6:00pm, and Saturday 8:30am – 1:00pm.
