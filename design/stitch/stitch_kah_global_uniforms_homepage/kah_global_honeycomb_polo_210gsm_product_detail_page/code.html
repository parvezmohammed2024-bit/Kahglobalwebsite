Enterprise Grade Technical Specifications

Engineered specifically for Malaysian corporate fleets, service technicians, and administrative teams. The 210 GSM honeycomb pique structure combines the breathable softness of long-staple combed cotton with the structural durability and wrinkle-resistance of technical polyester.

  • check_circle Composition: 65% Combed Cotton / 35% Durable Polyester Honeycomb Pique.
  • check_circle Weight: True 210 GSM (grams per square meter) heavyweight structure. Non-see-through guarantee.
  • check_circle Colour Fastness: Grade 4+ reactive dye process. Zero colour bleeding or fading through 50 industrial washes.
  • check_circle Collar & Cuffs: Anti-roll double-needle collar ribbing engineered to remain flat without curling.
  • check_circle Placket: Reinforced 3-button placket with cross-stitched shatterproof synthetic buttons.
